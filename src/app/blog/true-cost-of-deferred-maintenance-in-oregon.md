# The True Cost of Deferred Maintenance in Oregon: A Data-Driven Analysis

Don't let small problems become expensive disasters. This guide breaks down the real costs of delaying home maintenance in Oregon, using state-specific data to help you make informed decisions about protecting your most valuable asset.

## The High Cost of Procrastination

Delaying essential home maintenance is a gamble, and the odds are not in your favor. What starts as a minor issue can quickly escalate into a major expense. Here's a look at some of the most common—and costly—examples of deferred maintenance in Oregon:

### 1. Leaky Roofs: From Drip to Disaster

A small leak in your roof might not seem like a big deal, but it can lead to catastrophic damage if left unaddressed.

- **Initial Cost of Repair:** A simple patch or flashing repair can cost as little as **$300 - $500**.
- **Cost of Deferred Maintenance:** A persistent leak can lead to rotted sheathing, mold growth in your attic, and even structural damage to your rafters. A full roof replacement in Oregon can cost anywhere from **$10,000 to $25,000 or more**, depending on the size and materials.

### 2. Failing Gutters: The Silent Foundation Killer

Clogged or damaged gutters can cause water to overflow and saturate the ground around your foundation, leading to serious and expensive problems.

- **Initial Cost of Repair:** Professional gutter cleaning typically costs between **$150 - $250**.
- **Cost of Deferred Maintenance:** Water intrusion can cause your foundation to shift, crack, and even fail. Foundation repairs are among the most expensive home repairs, with costs often ranging from **$5,000 to $50,000**.

### 3. HVAC Neglect: When Your Comfort System Fails

Your heating and cooling system is the heart of your home's comfort. Neglecting regular maintenance can lead to a complete system failure, often during the hottest or coldest days of the year.

- **Initial Cost of Repair:** An annual HVAC tune-up costs around **$100 - $200**.
- **Cost of Deferred Maintenance:** A complete HVAC system replacement in Oregon can cost between **$7,000 and $15,000**.
