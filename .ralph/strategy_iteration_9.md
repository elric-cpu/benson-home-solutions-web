# Iteration 9: Off-Page SEO & Digital Authority Strategy

## 1. Backlink Profile Objective: "Trust by Association"
We prioritize relevance and authority over raw volume. One link from the **Oregon CCB** is worth 1,000 generic "guest posts."

## 2. Tier 1: Authority Anchors (High DA / Gov / Edu)
*   **Tactics:**
    *   **CCB & Industry Directories:** Ensure full profile optimization on `oregon.gov/ccb` and local business registries.
    *   **The "Methodology" Pitch:** Share the `TrueCostCalculator` data sources with Oregon university real estate programs (e.g., OSU, UO) as a teaching resource.
    *   **Government Data Feedback:** Provide feedback to FEMA or HUD on their public data APIs (via technical forums). This often results in a profile link or mention in "Community Contributors."

## 3. Tier 2: Regional & Niche Relevance
*   **Tactics:**
    *   **The "Embeddable ROI" Play:** Offer local Mid-Willamette Valley real estate agents an embeddable version of the `Maintenance ROI Calc` for their "Maintenance Resources" pages.
    *   **Harney County Community News:** Sponsor or contribute technical building tips to the *Burns Times-Herald* online edition.
    *   **Church/HOA Association Hubs:** Provide "Facility Audit Checklists" to regional church leadership portals.

## 4. Tier 3: Digital PR & Outreach
*   **Tactics:**
    *   **Expert Sourcing (HARO/Connectively):** Respond to daily queries regarding "Home Maintenance," "Rising Construction Costs," and "Smart Home Failures."
    *   **Podcast Guesting:** Target local business and real estate podcasts (e.g., "The Oregon Real Estate Podcast").
    *   **Visual PR:** Share high-fidelity project "Before/After" videos on LinkedIn and tag local suppliers (e.g., Parr Lumber, Milgard).

## 5. Defensive Off-Page (Brand Protection)
*   **Monitoring:** Use Google Alerts for "Benson Home Solutions" and "CCB 258533."
*   **Unlinked Mentions:** Reach out to sites mentioning our tools or case studies without a link and request a proper attribution link.
*   **Link Reclamation:** Monitor for broken external links to our old site and ensure 301 redirects are capturing that equity.

## 6. Local Signals (The "GEO" Authority)
*   **Citation Audit:** Total NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across Google, Apple Maps, Bing, and Yelp.
*   **Local Event Participation:** Documenting local site assessments and sponsorships via social signals that link back to geographic landing pages.
