# Iteration 10: Local SEO & Map Pack Dominance Strategy

## 1. Dual-Zone Optimization Model
We have two distinct markets with vastly different search behaviors.

### Zone 1: Mid-Willamette Valley (Albany, Salem, Corvallis)
*   **Search Behavior:** Competitive, price-sensitive, mobile-heavy.
*   **Objective:** Out-rank legacy firms on technical authority and response speed.

### Zone 2: High Desert / Harney County (Burns, Riley, Drewsey)
*   **Search Behavior:** Service-starved, search for "who shows up," values local identity.
*   **Objective:** Be the *only* visible digital option for rural repairs and demolition.

## 2. Google My Business (GMB / GBP) Optimization
The GMB profile is the most important "page" for local conversion.
*   **Primary Category:** "Property Maintenance" (Primary) | "General Contractor" (Secondary).
*   **Service Areas:** 
    *   Explicitly list all 10+ towns in the 75-mile radius of 97386.
    *   Explicitly list Harney County.
*   **Photo Strategy:**
    *   Upload 3-5 technical "Before/After" photos weekly.
    *   Geo-tag photos at the actual project site (Albany, Burns, etc.).
*   **GMB Posts:** 
    *   Post 2x weekly. Zero fluff. 
    *   "Senior Principal Alert: Found moisture ingress in [City] today. Here's what to look for."
*   **Reviews:** 
    *   Automate review requests post-service-log sign-off.
    *   Respond to every review with technical specifics (e.g., "Glad we could fix that deflection issue for you in Salem").

## 3. Local Citation & Directory Alignment
*   **Master NAP:** "Benson Home Solutions | (541) 321-5115 | 97386 + Service Area."
*   **Aggregators:** Use a tool (e.g., BrightLocal or Whitespark) to clean up citations on Yelp, YellowPages, and Angi.
*   **Niche Citations:** 
    *   Oregon Construction Contractors Board (CCB) Directory.
    *   Albany Chamber of Commerce.
    *   Harney County Business Directory.

## 4. Hyper-Local Landing Pages (The "GEO" Hub)
Build dedicated spoke pages for major towns: `/areas/[town-name]`.
*   **Content Checklist for each page:**
    *   H1: "[Town Name] Home Maintenance & Restoration Services"
    *   Local Project Gallery (Filtered by city).
    *   Local Review Snippets.
    *   Local Soil/Climate Insights (e.g., "Corvallis clay soil foundation risks").
    *   Embed Google Map of the specific town.

## 5. Map Pack Signals (On-Page)
*   **LocalBusiness Schema:** Embed JSON-LD with `areaServed` coordinates.
*   **City-Specific Metadata:** Meta titles like "Licensed Contractor Albany OR | Benson Home Solutions."
*   **Internal Links:** Link from `/areas/albany` to the `SubscriptionRecommender` with city-specific anchor text.
