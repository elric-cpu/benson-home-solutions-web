# Iteration 8: Authoritative Content Blueprint (The Backlink Magnet)

## 1. The "Forensic Authority" Content Framework
To attract high-quality backlinks (.gov, .edu, associations), our content must be **Defensive**, **Deterministic**, and **Documented**.

### Defensive Content (The "Anti-Scam" Guard)
*   **Goal:** Protect homeowners from common industry shortcuts.
*   **Topic:** "The CCB Audit: How to verify a contractor's bond history before signing."
*   **Backlink Potential:** Local news, consumer protection blogs.

### Deterministic Content (The Data Source)
*   **Goal:** Provide the data others are too lazy to find.
*   **Topic:** "2026 Oregon Residential Building Code: A layman's guide to the new energy efficiency requirements."
*   **Backlink Potential:** Real estate agents, architects, HVAC specialists.

### Documented Content (The Evidence)
*   **Goal:** Show, don't tell.
*   **Topic:** "Forensic Analysis of Window Seal Failure: Why 2015-2018 builds are leaking now."
*   **Backlink Potential:** Home inspection associations, moisture mitigation forums.

## 2. The Methodology Hub (Core Authority Asset)
This is our "Wikipedia" for property data. Every calculator result links back here.
*   **Property Tax Methodology:** Explaining Census ACS Table B25103.
*   **Maintenance Risk Methodology:** Citation of Harvard JCHS reports.
*   **Energy Benchmarking:** Analysis of DOE ResStock data.
*   **Impact:** Position Benson as a **Data Provider**, not just a contractor.

## 3. High-Engagement Formatting
*   **Visual Data:** CSS-only charts showing cost escalations. No bulky PNGs.
*   **Technical Callouts:** Highlighting specific code sections (e.g., "Per IRC R311.7.5...").
*   **The "Gus" Verdict:** Every technical brief ends with a blunt, urgent summary from our Senior Diagnostics Specialist persona.

## 4. Backlink Attraction Tactics (Natural Earned Links)
*   **The "Original Research" Play:** Publish an annual "State of Oregon Home Maintenance" report using anonymized data from the `TrueCostCalculator`.
*   **The "Calculated Quote" Play:** Create an embeddable widget of our ROI calculator for local real estate blogs.
*   **The "Expert Comment" Play:** Use HARO (Help A Reporter Out) to provide technical construction expertise for national housing stories.

## 5. Quality Control (The Ralph Audit)
Every piece of content must pass:
1.  **Technical Accuracy:** Verified by Elric or Gus.
2.  **Unique Data Point:** Must contain one number or code citation not found on page 1 of Google.
3.  **Actionable CTA:** Must lead to a Tool or Quote moment.
