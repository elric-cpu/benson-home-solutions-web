# Iteration 12: Trust, Credibility & Authority (Anti-Jerry Strategy)

## 1. Radical Transparency: The "Forensic" Standard
Trust is built when the client knows exactly *how* and *why* a decision is made.
*   **The Service Log Audit:** Clients have 24/7 access to their property’s digital service log. Every bolt turned is documented.
*   **The "Methodology" Shield:** We never pull numbers "out of thin air." Every estimate links to `/methodology`, citing FEMA, HUD, and 2026 PPI data.
*   **No-Emoji Policy:** In professional reports and technical briefs, we use zero emojis. We communicate like Principal Engineers, not social media influencers.

## 2. Institutional Proof (The "Big Three")
*   **CCB #258533:** Displayed in the footer of every page, the header of every agreement, and the signature block of every report.
*   **Senior Principal Engines:** Our tools are branded as "2026 Senior Principal Engines" to signal high-fidelity data over "ballpark" guesses.
*   **IRC Compliance:** We explicitly reference the International Residential Code (IRC) 2026 in our diagnostic reports to show code-level expertise.

## 3. Social Proof (The Authority Loop)
Generic testimonials ("They were great!") are weak. We use **Structured Technical Testimonials**.
*   **Format:** [Client Type] + [Problem] + [Diagnostic] + [Outcome].
    *   *Example:* "Church Board Member: We had recurring moisture ingress. Benson diagnosed hydrostatic pressure using forensic sensors. Their maintenance subscription stopped the failure and saved us $12k in rebuild costs."
*   **Trust Bar:** Logo slider of local suppliers (Parr Lumber, Milgard) and associations (Albany Chamber, Harney County Business).

## 4. Security & Data Integrity
*   **Secure Agreement Versioning:** We use PandaDoc for tamper-evident digital signatures and document versioning.
*   **Data Privacy:** Clear privacy policy at `/privacy` explaining how property data is used (and that it is *never* sold to lead aggregators).
*   **HTTPS/HSTS:** Bank-level transport security for the Client Portal.
