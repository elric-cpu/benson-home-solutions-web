# Hiring Plan (Budget-Conscious)

## Team Models

### Ideal Model (14 specialists)
- Fastest throughput, highest quality, lowest role-overload risk
- Best for concurrent delivery across SEO, product, and growth streams

### Minimum Viable Model (7–8 people)
Use the approved combinations:
- **Senior SEO Strategist:** roles 1 + 4 + 5
- **AEO/GEO + Schema Specialist:** roles 2 + 3
- **Full-Stack Developer:** roles 7 + 8
- **UX + CRO Specialist:** roles 6 + 13
- Keep dedicated: 9, 10, 11, 12, 14 (or contract support where needed)

## Risk Controls for Combined Roles
- **SEO + backlinks bundle:** enforce ethics reviews by 14 to prevent manipulative tactics
- **Frontend + backend bundle:** protect performance time budget; QA (12) must remain independent
- **UX + CRO bundle:** require experiment design reviews and data validation checkpoints

## Hiring Priority Order
1. **14 (PM/Tech Lead)** — governance and sequencing first
2. **1/4/5 bundle** + **2/3 bundle** — fix demand-gen foundation
3. **10 + 11 + 6** — trust and conversion assets
4. **7/8 + 9** — implementation and intelligence layer
5. **12 + 13** — reliability and growth loop

## Decision Rules
- Never combine roles 5 and 14 in one person
- Do not staff QA (12) under implementation owners (7/8)
- Final copy authority remains human trade-experienced writer (10)
