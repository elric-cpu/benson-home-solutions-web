# Benson Home Solutions — 2026 SEO, AEO, & SGO Strategy

## 1. Executive Summary
In 2026, search has evolved beyond traditional keyword matching. We are now optimizing for **Answer Engines (AEO)** and **Search Generative Experiences (SGO)**. Our goal is to position Benson Home Solutions as the Mid-Valley's No-Nonsense Maintenance Specialists. We win on diagnostic truth and transparency.

## 2. Key Pillars

### A. Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
- **Answer-First Architecture:** Every service page leads with a 2-3 sentence diagnostic answer based on systems age and field reality (e.g., "Water heaters last 5 years now, not 15").
- **Structured Data (JSON-LD):** Extensive use of `Service`, `LocalBusiness`, `FAQPage`, and `HowTo` schema.
- **Speakable Specification:** Markup for voice assistants (Siri, Alexa, Google).

### B. Search Generative Optimization (SGO)
- **Entity Consistency:** Ensuring Benson Home Solutions is recognized as a single entity across CCB, GBP, and the web.
- **Data Anchors:** Using factual, lived experiences (e.g., "6.5' x 8.5' picture window," "30% gross margin").
- **Citations:** Linking to authoritative sources (CCB, IICRC, FEMA) to build trust without fluff.

### C. Voice Search & Conversational AI
- **Natural Language Keywords:** Shifting from "kitchen remodel Albany" to "Who is the best contractor for a kitchen remodel near Albany?"
- **Diagnostic Content:** "What to do if your pipes freeze in Sweet Home" — targeting urgent, spoken queries.
