# Benson Home Solutions: Marketing & Branding (Human-Readable)

Welcome to the Benson Home Solutions marketing guide. This document outlines our brand's visual identity and guidelines for creating new materials.

## 1. Our Brand Identity
*   **Legal Entity**: Benson Enterprises
*   **DBA (Client-Facing)**: Benson Home Solutions
*   **Tone**: Confident, direct, and professional. No emojis in client-facing copy.
*   **Voice**: Direct contractor voice (Elric Benson).

## 2. Visual Standards
*   **Primary Color**: Oxblood / Maroon (#4C0C14). This represents our heritage and strength.
*   **Accent Color**: Cream (#FFFDF9). Provides a clean, professional contrast.
*   **Secondary Colors**: Charcoal (#2D2D2D) and Slate (#4A4A4A).
*   **Typography**:
    *   **Print/Marketing Materials**: Calibri.
    *   **Website**: Source Sans 3 (professional web equivalent).

## 3. Creating Marketing Materials
When creating flyers, brochures, or digital ads:
1.  **Layout**: Use a logo-left, contact-info-right header.
2.  **Dividers**: Use thin maroon (#4C0C14) lines for section separation.
3.  **Trust Signals**: Always include our Oregon CCB license: **CCB #258533**.
4.  **CTA**: Direct the client to call our primary office: **(541) 321-5115**.

## 4. Digital Marketing Strategy
*   **Local SEO**: Every service page targets a specific keyword for the Mid-Willamette Valley or Harney County.
*   **Authority**: We build authority by linking to 6 trusted resources on every page. These should include governmental bodies (FEMA, NOAA) or local municipal resources.
*   **Lead Capture**: All marketing efforts should lead back to a HubSpot-powered form on our website.
