# Benson Home Solutions: Content Management Guide (Human-Readable)

Welcome to the Benson Home Solutions content management system. This guide provides clear, non-technical instructions for maintaining our digital presence.

## 1. Managing Customer Reviews (FAQs & Testimonials)
Our reputation is built on reliability. We use real customer questions and testimonials to build trust.

### To Add an Actual Customer Question (FAQ):
1.  **Open Sanity Studio** and navigate to **FAQ Items**.
2.  **Create a New Item.** Enter the question exactly as it was asked by the customer.
3.  **Enable "Actual Customer Question"**: Check the box to ensure the "Verified" badge appears on the website.
4.  **Tag the Source**: Record whether it came from Email, Phone, or an in-person conversation.
5.  **Assign to Services**: Select the related services so the question appears on those specific pages.

### To Add a Testimonial:
1.  Navigate to **Testimonials** in Sanity.
2.  **Add Client Name and Quote.** Keep quotes concise but impactful.
3.  **Assign a Rating (1-5 stars).** We aim for transparency.
4.  **Relate to a Service**: This ensures the review shows up where it's most relevant.

## 2. Adding Visual Media (Pictures & Videos)
High-fidelity visual evidence is our best marketing tool.

### Adding Hero Videos:
*   **Format**: Use high-quality MP4 files.
*   **Process**: Upload the video to a reliable CDN or cloud storage, then copy the **direct MP4 link** into the "Hero Video URL" field on the desired page (Homepage, Service Page, etc.).
*   **Backgrounds**: Ensure the video is professional and cinematic. It should represent the quality of our work.

### Adding Project Pictures:
1.  Navigate to **Projects** in Sanity.
2.  **Upload "Before" and "After" images.** These are critical for showing the value we provide.
3.  **Add Alt Text**: Describe the image clearly for accessibility and SEO (e.g., "After water damage restoration in Albany, OR").

## 3. Managing Marketing Materials & Resources
Every page must have **exactly 6 authoritative resources** to maintain our high SEO standards.

### To Update Resources:
1.  On any Page document (Homepage, Service Page, etc.), scroll to the **Resources & Backlinks** section.
2.  **Add 6 Items**: Each item should include:
    *   **Title**: Clear name of the resource (e.g., FEMA Flood Hazard Layer).
    *   **URL**: Direct link to the source.
    *   **Authority Label**: Who provided this? (e.g., FEMA, City of Salem, CCB).
    *   **Is Backlink**: Check this if we are specifically tracking a verified backlink for SEO purposes.
