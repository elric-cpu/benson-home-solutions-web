All written materials use Elric Benson's authentic voice — confident, direct, and authoritative. Content should sound like it came from Elric, not from a marketing agency.

### Voice Patterns

#### How Elric Talks to Customers

1. **Diagnoses before selling.** On an intake call about water damage, the first thing out of his mouth is an investigative question: *"Is there a bathroom above there?"* He's solving the problem before he's sold the job.
2. **Meets customers where they are.** *"Text it to me if that works."* Phone-first, not form-first. Doesn't force people into a workflow — adapts to theirs.
3. **Emergency = immediate action, zero fluff.** *"Get me your address, text it to me, dehumidifiers today, tear-out in the morning."* No script, no scheduling link, no "let me check our availability."

#### How Elric Talks About Quality & Price

1. **Honest about price trade-offs.** *"You end up with a way nicer kitchen but it's also reflected in the price."* Doesn't hide cost implications of quality work.
2. **Leads with family credibility naturally.** *"My dad has been a custom cabinet builder for forty years."* Not a marketing bullet — just context that explains why the work is better.
3. **No apology, no justification on pricing — just business reality.** *"Our number reflects our operating costs and that we have to turn a profit in order to stay in business."*
4. **Transparent on margins when asked.** *"We can provide a breakdown on request but our margin is at 30% gross so under 15% net."* Treats the customer like an adult.

#### How Elric Talks About the Work

1. **Systems-age truth-teller.** *"I wish people understood systems age and that nothing lasts as long — water heaters are 5 years now instead of 15."*
2. **Maintenance is cheaper than surprise repair.** This is the core belief and the business's core pitch.
3. **Proud of functional impact, not flashy renovations.** The projects he's proudest of are an ADA ramp and a picture window replacing a leaky bay window — both solve real problems.
4. **Specific dimensions and details over vague claims.** *"6.5' x 8.5' picture window," "full energy package."* Never *"a beautiful new window."*
5. **Performance described as felt experience.** *"No temperature change walking past it."* Not *"energy efficient"* — tells you what it *feels* like to live with the result.
6. **Lets the work speak for itself.** *"There's pictures on our Facebook."* Doesn't oversell — points you to the evidence.

#### How Elric Thinks About the Website

1. **Emergency UX = phone numbers front and center.** No menu hunting, no chatbot intercepting.
2. **Dual-action contact: call AND text simultaneously.** Clicking either number should trigger both a phone call and a text.
3. **Second phone number for after-hours.** (541) 413-0480 is the emergency/afterhours line. Two numbers = redundancy for when it matters most.

#### How Elric Sees the Business

1. **Identity = maintenance specialists, not general contractors.** Leads with maintenance, not remodeling or construction.
2. **Differentiates on tooling.** *"Interior concrete saws, dehumidifiers, the equipment most contractors don't carry."* Owns the equipment others rent or sub out.

### Writing Rules

#### Do

- Use specific numbers, dimensions, and product details instead of adjectives
- Describe results as *felt experiences* ("no temperature change walking past it")
- Write like you're talking to the customer across a kitchen table
- Include real trade-off language ("nicer kitchen, but it's reflected in the price")
- Use investigative questions as a content pattern ("Is there a bathroom above there?")
- Show the math — margins, cost ranges, what drives the number

#### Don't

- Use "we're committed to excellence" or any variation
- Use "your trusted partner" or "dedicated team"
- Hide behind "call for a quote" — show ranges and explain what moves the number
- Write emergency content that puts a form between the customer and a phone number
- Use "I'd be happy to help" — Elric doesn't talk like that
- Describe the business as a general contractor — it's maintenance specialists with specialized tools
