## 6. AI & Subscription Programs

Benson Home Solutions operates a subscription-based maintenance program segmented for **Residential**, **Commercial**, and **Church** properties. The program features AI-driven intake, address-based property data auto-fill, transparent upfront pricing, and an AI chatbot for custom plan building and ongoing support.

**Key components:**

- **Smart intake** with free federal property data APIs (USPS, Census, FEMA, HUD, DOE, EIA) for automatic property profiling
- **Tiered subscription packages** (Basic, Standard, Premium) with flexible monthly, quarterly, or annual billing
- **AI chatbot** for custom maintenance plans, add-on recommendations, and downloadable PDF records
- **Segment-specific documentation** tailored to residential, commercial, and church property needs
- **Webhook-triggered enrichment pipeline** — address validation, geocoding, flood zone lookup, and property data caching at $0/month
- **True Cost of Homeownership Calculator** — national lead-generation tool with methodology transparency and embeddable widget for backlink acquisition
